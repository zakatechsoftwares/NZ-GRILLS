Delete your Zak Canteen account

This page explains how to delete your Zak Canteen account and its data.

In the app

  1. Open Zak Canteen and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email zakatechsoftware@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Zak Canteen account", sent from the email address you signed up with. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login, including Google sign-in access
  • Your profile: name, email and phone number
  • Saved delivery addresses and pinned locations
  • Favorites, reviews, notifications and notification settings

What is kept

Past orders are kept for sales records with your name, delivery address, delivery notes and review text removed, so they can no longer be linked to you. Nothing else is kept.
