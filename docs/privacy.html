Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Zak Canteen Android app ("the app") collects, how it is used and shared, and how you can delete it. The app is developed by Zakatech ("we", "us"). Contact: zakatechsoftware@gmail.com.

The app is a portfolio demo. Payments run in test mode, so no real money is charged and no food is delivered.

Data we collect

DataWhy
Name, email address and phone number To create and manage your account and contact you about orders. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
Delivery addresses To deliver orders. If you choose "Use my current location", we save that location with the address so couriers can find you.
Orders, delivery notes, ratings, reviews and favorites To process orders, show your order history and let you rate orders and save favorite items.
Courier location (couriers only) While a courier is delivering an order, their device location is shared with that order's customer for live tracking. It is only collected during an active delivery, while the app is open, and is cleared when the order is delivered.
Push notification token and notification preferences To send order updates and, if you allow them, promotions. You can turn these off in Profile → Notification Preferences.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Flutterwave. Your card details are entered on Flutterwave's payment page and are never stored by the app. We send Flutterwave your name, email, phone number and the order amount, and we receive a transaction reference to confirm the payment.

Who can see your data

  • Staff and admins see orders and the customer's name, email, phone and delivery address so they can prepare and dispatch them.
  • Couriers see the delivery address and contact details for orders assigned to them. Customers see the name and phone number of the courier on their order.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf:
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in
    • Google: Google sign-in and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications
    • Expo: push notification delivery
    • Flutterwave: payments

We may also disclose data if required by law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access rules that limit each user to the data their role needs.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account permanently removes your login, profile, saved addresses, favorites, reviews, notifications and notification settings. Past orders are kept for sales records with your name, delivery address, delivery notes and review text removed, so they can no longer be linked to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: zakatechsoftware@gmail.com
