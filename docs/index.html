Zak Canteen: Food Ordering Demo

This is a portfolio demo. No real money is charged and no food is delivered. Payments run in Flutterwave's test mode.

Zak Canteen is an Android app that shows a complete food ordering system for a canteen or restaurant, built by Zakatech. It has four roles:

Customers browse the menu, order with card or cash on delivery, save delivery addresses, track their order and rate it.

Staff receive orders, update their status and assign couriers.

Couriers take delivery offers and share their location with the customer while delivering.

Admins manage the menu, categories, staff, promotions and sales analytics.
